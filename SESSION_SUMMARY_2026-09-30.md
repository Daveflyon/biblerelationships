## Work Completed

### Phase 1: Lessons 11 & 12 Content Enhancements

#### Lesson 11 — "Being Unequally Yoked"

**Update 1: Core Truth Enhancement**
- **Location:** Core Truth section
- **Change:** Expanded to emphasize "covenant with Christ Himself"
- **Added:** "At its heart, this is about covenant with Christ Himself. You have pledged allegiance to Jesus as Lord; your spouse has not. You cannot walk the same direction spiritually."
- **Why:** Clarifies that the issue is fundamental spiritual incompatibility at the level of covenant commitment

**Update 2: Hebrews 7:10 Reframe**
- **Location:** Bible Teaching section, "For Those Already in an Unequally Yoked Marriage"
- **Changed:** Replaced metaphorical Levi/Abraham example with direct theological statement
- **New Text:** "Hebrews 7:10 shows how God honours faith across generations. Levi participated in Abraham's covenant faithfulness though unborn. Similarly, your faith in Christ has a covering power for your household that extends beyond your spouse's current belief. This is not a guarantee that conflict will disappear, but a promise that your faithfulness matters and is not wasted."
- **Why:** Clearer theology; states covering promise explicitly

**Update 3: 6th Discussion Question**
- **Location:** Group Discussion Questions section
- **New Question:** "What would it look like to speak clearly about your faith to an unbelieving spouse without being preachy or condemning? Where's the line between faithful witness and pressure?"
- **Why:** Addresses practical witness challenge

#### Lesson 12 — "Putting It Together"

**Update: Christ-Connection Paragraph**
- **Location:** Bible Teaching section, end
- **New Paragraph:** "Throughout these twelve lessons, you have seen Christ repeatedly. In why marriage exists, He is the model of covenant love. In the roles of husband and wife, His leadership and the Church's response are reflected. In the priority of your marriage, His bond with His people is mirrored. As you step into your real life, keep seeing your marriage as a picture of something bigger than yourselves."
- **Why:** Theological capstone to entire twelve-lesson arc
